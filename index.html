Questions investors ask

Clear answers,before you commit.

Anything else on your mind? Your advisor will answer it on the first call.

Can I buy commercial property in Dubai from India?

Yes. Resident Indians can remit up to USD 250,000 per person per financial year under RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme, which permits buying residential or commercial property abroad. Family members can pool their limits, and we guide you through the remittance with your bank.

How are my payments protected?

For off-plan purchases, Dubai law requires every project to have its own escrow account at an approved bank. Your instalments go into that account, not to the developer, and are released only against verified construction progress. We check the project's registration and escrow details before you pay a single dirham.

What is the minimum investment?

Investment options, pricing and the payment schedule are set out in the project specifications. Request them and your advisor will walk you through each option.

Does it qualify me for a UAE Golden Visa?

Owning property worth AED 2 million or more can make you eligible for the 10-year renewable Golden Visa, including off-plan property under current rules. Final eligibility is decided by UAE authorities.

What returns can I expect?

Returns depend on the asset, location, use and price, and are never guaranteed. Where a commercial use and income model is established, it is shared with the project information so you can judge it yourself.

Do I need to travel to Dubai?

No. Booking, documentation and transfer can all be handled remotely. Many clients like to visit before deciding, and we arrange private viewings when you do.

Is there a fee to enquire?

None. Project information and the advisory call are free and carry no obligation.
